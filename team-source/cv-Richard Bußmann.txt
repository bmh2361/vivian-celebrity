Dr Richard Bußmann serves as Chief Financial Officer (CFO) of Vivian Adventure.  He has extensive knowledge of the European market, particularly in the areas of corporate compliance and mergers and acquisitions, and assists Chinese companies in entering the European market. Prior roles included real estate credit investments as Vice President (VP) at LCN Capital Partners and Spark Capital, as well as corporate strategy, M&A, and alternative asset investment at ARAG SE. 

Richard has a deep understanding of the Chinese market, holds a PhD in Applied Financial Mathematics from Central China Normal University in Wuhan and speaks fluent Mandarin, while having led business projects in mainland China, granting him an exceptional insight into the culture and commercial landscape of the Chinese community.
